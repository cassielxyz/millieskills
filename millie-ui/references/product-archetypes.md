# Product & Surface Archetypes

## SaaS / AI Marketing
Needs: comprehension, evidence, trust, conversion.
Good: refined, editorial SaaS, technical precision, controlled spatial.
Avoid: fake metrics, generic gradient blobs, floating dashboard collage by reflex.

## Corporate / B2B
Needs: trust, capability, evidence, navigation.
Good: Swiss, refined minimal, editorial.
Motion: restrained.

## Portfolio / Agency
Needs: identity, work, storytelling.
Good: editorial, anti-grid, maximalist, cinematic, immersive.
Rule: the work remains easier to access than the effect.

## Ecommerce Discovery
Needs: product discovery, comparison, desire.
Can be expressive.
Checkout becomes calmer and more conventional.

## Luxury / Fashion
Needs: desire, imagery, material quality.
Good: luxury minimal, editorial luxe, cinematic, heritage.
Avoid: fake gold and cursive clichés.

## Food / Restaurant
Needs: appetite, menu, hours, location, booking/order.
Good: crafted, editorial, organic, graphic.
Do not hide practical information.

## Travel / Hospitality
Needs: inspiration + practical planning/booking.
Good: cinematic, editorial, organic, luxury.
Avoid full-screen media blocking action.

## Real Estate / Architecture
Needs: spatial understanding, imagery, details, inquiry.
Good: Swiss, editorial, refined, 3D showcase.
Keep property facts legible.

## News / Publication
Needs: scan, read, navigate, recirculate.
Good: editorial, Swiss.
Motion: low.

## Documentation
Needs: search, hierarchy, code/readability.
Good: precision, utility.
Avoid marketing spacing.

## Government / Public Service
Needs: completion, clarity, accessibility.
Novelty: low.
Avoid experimental navigation.

## Cybersecurity Marketing
Needs: trust, technical credibility, real evidence.
Good: data precision, industrial, restrained dark/spatial.
Avoid Matrix rain, fake terminal, neon-green cliché.

## Dashboard / Admin
Needs: scanning, filters, state, actions.
Density: compact/balanced.
Avoid giant stat cards, decorative bento.

## SOC / NOC / Monitoring
Needs: alert hierarchy, freshness, scope, telemetry.
Good: data-dense precision.
Motion: live state only.
Avoid glow overload.

## Finance / Banking
Needs: trust, exact amounts, reversibility, secure comprehension.
Motion: restrained.
Critical flows: predictable.

## Healthcare
Needs: calm, readability, accessibility, state clarity.
Avoid low-contrast pastel dependence.

## Education
Needs: comprehension, progress, motivation.
Good: friendly/graphic/tactile accents.
Do not gamify every action.

## Music / Audio
Needs: artwork, playback, tactile control.
Good: modern skeuomorphic accents, image-derived palettes, cinematic.
Motion: progress/waveform/tactile feedback.

## Video / Media
Needs: content dominance + usable controls.
Good: dark cinematic, selective glass controls.

## Messaging / Social
Needs: fast high-frequency interaction and presence.
Motion: quick state feedback, not slow navigation.

## Maps / Travel App
Needs: spatial context + controls.
Good: layered spatial, selective glass.
Do not cover the map with opaque chrome unnecessarily.

## Smart Home / IoT
Needs: status, clear control, environment.
Good: tactile/refined spatial.
Avoid ambiguous shadow-only toggle state.

## Developer Tool
Needs: density, keyboard, code/data hierarchy.
Good: utility/precision.
Do not fake a terminal if it is not one.

## Creative Tool / Editor
Needs: canvas dominance, pro controls.
Use expressive visuals in the content/canvas, not decorative toolbars.

## Mobile Consumer
Needs: touch comfort, platform navigation, identity.
Never shrink a desktop web layout.

## Tablet / Foldable Pro
Needs: adaptive panes, keyboard/stylus, resizable windows.

## Desktop Professional
Needs: shortcuts, windows/panes, precise density.

## Wearable
Needs: glanceability and low interaction burden.

## Spatial/XR
Needs: comfort, field-of-view, depth, gaze/gesture alternatives.
Avoid transporting a flat website into space.

# Creative Motion Vocabulary

Creative motion is selected by product meaning and frequency.

## Micro/tactile
- press compression;
- spring settle;
- magnetic attraction (rare/desktop-oriented);
- slider elasticity;
- drag inertia;
- hold/progress affordance;
- toggle morph;
- notification insertion/removal;
- number/value interpolation.

## Type
- mask reveal;
- per-word/per-line reveal;
- variable-font axis motion;
- scramble with readable final state;
- marquee/ticker;
- path-following labels.

Avoid character-by-character animation on long body copy.

## Surface/material
- glare/reflection;
- light sweep;
- gradient reflection;
- chrome/holographic response;
- refraction/distortion;
- spotlight;
- grain/noise drift;
- liquid/tactile morph.

Keep text contrast stable; do not animate aggressive material behind critical copy.

## Layout
- shared-layout morph;
- card -> detail expansion;
- tab indicator continuity;
- dock/dynamic-island-like morph only when function warrants;
- sortable/reorder continuity;
- master-detail transition.

## Cursor/pointer
Custom cursors, trails and magnetic interactions are optional desktop embellishments. Preserve
normal pointer meaning, touch behavior and reduced-motion settings; never make navigation depend on
them.

## Physics
Use springs/physics when they communicate direct manipulation or object continuity. Do not add
bounciness to serious high-frequency controls merely because it looks lively.

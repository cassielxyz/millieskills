# Visual Style Catalog

Choose one primary style and normally at most one supporting influence.

## Structural / Refined

### Refined Minimal
DNA: precise alignment, quiet surfaces, controlled accent, type-led hierarchy.
Best: SaaS, B2B, architecture, finance, productivity.
Motion: low-distance, precise.
Risk: "minimal" becoming empty or generic.

### Luxury Minimal
DNA: restrained typography, strong imagery/material detail, low chrome.
Best: fashion, hospitality, beauty, automotive, premium property.
Motion: controlled masks, image transitions, slow rare moments.
Risk: fake gold, weak beige contrast, decorative serif by reflex.

### Swiss / International
DNA: rational grid, asymmetry, typographic scale, alignment.
Best: culture, architecture, studios, information-heavy marketing.
Motion: directional/grid-aware.
Risk: collapsing into generic corporate minimalism.

### Editorial / Magazine
DNA: reading rhythm, art-directed images, captions, strong hierarchy.
Best: publishing, portfolio, reports, fashion, storytelling.
Motion: image/type masks, restrained sticky media.
Risk: unreadable display type.

### Heritage / Neo-classic
DNA: archival/print references, refined serif usage, crafted detail.
Best: museums, food, hospitality, institutional history.
Risk: nostalgia obscuring usability.

### Monochrome Precision
DNA: value/weight/border do hierarchy; optional single accent.
Best: developer tools, pro SaaS, docs.
Risk: insufficient state differentiation.

### Industrial / Utilitarian
DNA: exposed structure, condensed/technical type, strong rules.
Best: infrastructure, logistics, manufacturing, internal ops.
Risk: fake industrial texture.

### Data-Dense Precision
DNA: compact hierarchy, tabular numbers, status visibility.
Best: SOC/NOC, analytics, finance, operations.
Risk: dashboard-card mosaic.

## Material / Tactile

### Glassmorphism
DNA: translucent surface, blur, edge highlight, depth.
Best: overlays, navigation, media controls, controlled dashboards.
Risk: nested blur, unreadable text, GPU cost.

### Liquid-Glass-Inspired
DNA: dynamic translucent foreground controls, adaptive highlights, morphing.
Best: current Apple native controls/navigation; selective web interpretation.
Risk: coating content, copying Apple identity.

### Neumorphism / Soft UI
DNA: same-color surface, opposing shadow, inset/raised states.
Best: limited audio/wellness/control clusters.
Risk: poor affordance/contrast. Use hybrid boundaries/focus/accent.

### Claymorphism
DNA: puffy forms, soft shadows, friendly color.
Best: education, onboarding, playful consumer.
Risk: childish treatment in serious operations.

### Modern Skeuomorphism
DNA: selective physical metaphor and tactile control.
Best: audio, camera, automotive, creative tools.
Risk: realism unrelated to function.

### Paper / Crafted
DNA: print, fibers/noise, rules, tactile layering.
Best: editorial, food, culture, craft.
Risk: texture reducing legibility.

### Chrome / Holographic
DNA: spectral reflections, metallic material, controlled glow.
Best: fashion, music, gaming, launches.
Risk: moving spectral surfaces behind text.

## Graphic

### Neobrutalism
DNA: hard border, offset shadow, saturated block, chunky type.
Best: youth, creative, education, campaigns.
Risk: trust-heavy interfaces.

### Raw Brutalism
DNA: deliberate roughness, exposed structure, stark contrast.
Best: art/culture/experimental.
Risk: "broken" interaction disguised as style.

### Bauhaus / Geometric
DNA: simple geometry, bold composition, structural color.
Best: events, design, architecture.
Risk: decorative geometry detached from content.

### Art Deco / Geometric Luxury
DNA: symmetry, fine geometric framing, restrained rich accents.
Best: hospitality, heritage, events, luxury.
Risk: fake metallic decoration.

### Maximalism
DNA: layered scale/color/imagery with deliberate hierarchy.
Best: fashion, music, entertainment, campaign.
Risk: every element competing.

### Anti-grid / Expressive Type
DNA: intentional overlap/off-axis composition; hidden underlying structure.
Best: portfolios, fashion, campaigns.
Risk: mobile simply stacked instead of recomposed.

## Atmospheric / Cultural

### Organic
DNA: natural forms, earthy/color-derived palette, humanist type.
Best: food, wellness, sustainability, travel.
Risk: "green gradient = sustainable" cliché.

### Soft Graphic / Pastel
DNA: friendly geometry and lower-chroma color.
Best: education, consumer, wellness.
Risk: weak contrast/state differentiation.

### Dark Cinematic
DNA: tinted near-black, imagery, dramatic focal lighting.
Best: media, automotive, gaming, luxury.
Risk: neon cyan/purple default.

### Retro Futurist
DNA: historic-future materials, display type, spatial graphics.
Best: creative tech, music, portfolio.
Risk: "sci-fi" noise with no product relation.

### Y2K / Cyber-pop
DNA: glossy digital play, metallic accents, compact graphics.
Best: youth fashion, gaming, music.
Risk: critical enterprise workflow.

### Pixel / Game
DNA: pixel visuals, limited palette, game-state cues.
Best: games, community, creative portfolio.
Risk: accessibility and tiny targets.

### Fashion / Editorial Luxe
DNA: photography-first, dramatic type, sparse UI.
Best: fashion, beauty, photographers.
Risk: navigation disappearing behind art direction.

## Spatial / Immersive

### Layered Spatial
DNA: foreground/midground/background, perspective, floating surfaces.
Best: maps, media, product UI.
Risk: unreadable perspective.

### 3D Product Showcase
DNA: real-time object, camera-led product understanding.
Best: hardware, automotive, furniture, industrial.
Risk: huge assets for little value.

### WebGL Narrative
DNA: DOM semantics + GPU visuals, scene-like pacing.
Best: portfolio/campaign/entertainment.
Risk: inaccessible essential content in canvas.

### Scroll Cinematic
DNA: story state mapped to scroll progress.
Best: launches, case studies, product stories.
Risk: scroll hijacking/locked content.

### DOM + WebGL
DNA: semantic content stays DOM; spatial media in GPU layer.
Best: premium creative experience.
Risk: sync complexity/performance.
